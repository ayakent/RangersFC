01 The Club

Thirty years on our home pitch

Rangers Football Club was founded on 14 June 1996 in Barangay Mabuhay, General Santos City. For three decades the club has given children and young people a safe, positive place to learn football while developing discipline, teamwork, leadership, respect and perseverance.

Most of our players come from underprivileged families. Training happens on a simple, dusty field with well-worn equipment, and that has never stopped anyone. Rangers keeps producing passionate players who compete with pride, determination and sportsmanship.

More than a football club, Rangers FC is a family built by dedicated coaches, supportive parents, volunteers and community partners who believe that sport can transform lives and keep young people away from negative influences.

Our Mission

To develop children through football by instilling discipline, teamwork, sportsmanship, respect, leadership and resilience, while creating opportunities for every child regardless of economic background.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading grassroots football programmes in Mindanao, empowering young athletes to reach their full potential on and off the field while inspiring stronger, healthier communities.

03 What We Do

Football first, character always

Grassroots football development

A long-term pathway that welcomes every child, from first touch to competitive play.

Training for children and youth

Regular sessions building skills, fitness and confidence across age groups.

Local and regional tournaments

Match experience where players compete with pride and learn to win and lose well.

Character and leadership development

Discipline, respect and responsibility are coached as deliberately as passing.

Community outreach through sports

Using football to reach, gather and lift up young people in our barangay.

Parent and volunteer engagement

Families are part of every training, tournament and fundraiser. This club is a team effort.

Healthy and active lifestyles

Keeping kids moving, outdoors and away from negative influences.

Every child who dreams of playing football deserves the chance to learn, compete and grow, whatever their circumstances. Our goal since 1996

Our Community

A club carried by its people

Rangers FC is proudly supported by families from Barangay Mabuhay, San Isidro and neighbouring communities. Parents volunteer at trainings, tournaments, fundraising activities and community projects, which makes the club a true family driven organisation.
