02 Ordering

How this works

Step onePICK

Add what you want to your order. It is kept on this page only — nothing is sent anywhere yet.

Step twoSEND

Your list becomes a ready-written message. Send it by text, email, or copy it into Messenger.

Step threeCONFIRM

A coach replies with sizes, the total and where to collect. Payment is arranged directly with them.

Step fourWEAR IT

Collect at training. Every peso stays with the clubs — there is no middleman taking a cut.

Why there is no card checkout

This shop takes orders; it does not take payments. That is deliberate for now — a card gateway costs money every month and needs a registered business behind it, which is a lot of overhead for a grassroots club selling shirts to its own families.

So orders come straight to a coach, and payment is settled the way it already is at training. If the volume ever justifies proper online payment, it can be added without rebuilding any of this.

Every order does something

Most Rangers players come from families who cannot spend on football. Shirts sold to those who can are what put boots on the ones who cannot.

Buying a cap is not charity and it is not nothing. It is a pair of boots, eventually.
